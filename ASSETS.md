# Visual assets

All ten game assets in `public/assets/` were created for this project with the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool, inspected, and compressed to WebP. They are illustrative, not product-specific nutrition evidence. Local fonts and Phosphor icons are bundled through npm.

## Prompt summaries

- `hero.webp`: realistic still life matching the chosen welcome screen: red apple, clear water, wholemeal bread and magnifying glass, subjects focused to the right, no text or UI; edited with ImageGen to remove the background and preserve real alpha transparency.
- `badge.webp`: friendly gold circular achievement medallion, apple and magnifying glass, no text; edited with ImageGen to remove the background and preserve real alpha transparency.
- `wholemeal.webp`, `white-bread.webp`: separate studio food photographs of two slices of the named bread on clean off-white, soft diffuse light, no hands, cutlery or text.
- `brown-rice.webp`, `white-rice.webp`: separate photographs of cooked rice in a simple white bowl, matching light and composition, no garnish or text.
- `apple.webp`, `orange.webp`, `juice.webp`, `water.webp`: separate square studio photographs of the named food/drink, clear centered subject, off-white backdrop, no hands, labels or UI.

The visual target combines the selected light welcome, green two-choice question screen, and celebratory results screen. User-requested refinements remove exhibition/internal wording, use natural food names and keep the Fit+ link separate from the star invitation.
